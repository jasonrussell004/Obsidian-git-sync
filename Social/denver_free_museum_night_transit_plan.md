## Step-by-Step Itinerary

### Phase 1: Littleton to Denver Museum of Nature & Science (DMNS)
1. **RTD C Line Rail Northbound**
   * **Depart:** [Littleton / Downtown Station](https://app.rtd-denver.com/route/C/schedule) at **3:45 PM** (Train departs Littleton Downtown ~3:41–3:45 PM).
   * **Arrive:** [Denver Union Station](https://app.rtd-denver.com/route/C/schedule) at **~4:15–4:20 PM**.
2. **Transfer to RTD Route 20 Bus (Eastbound)**
   * **Depart:** Board Route 20 at **15th & Delgany St** or Union Station area at **~4:30 PM**.
   * **Arrive:** Alight at **Colorado Blvd & Montview Blvd** (or 20th Ave / Colorado Blvd) right along City Park at **~4:50 PM**.
   * **Visit:** Walk directly into [Denver Museum of Nature & Science](https://www.dmns.org). Free admission begins at 5:00 PM.

---

### Phase 2: DMNS to Golden Triangle (Art & History Museums)
*Take advantage of the complimentary event shuttle loops connecting central Denver museums from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM.*

1. **Board Free Event Shuttle**
   * Walk out the main entrance of DMNS around **7:45 PM – 8:00 PM**.
   * Board the shuttle loop serving the Denver Museum of Nature & Science.
2. **Arrive at Civic Center / Golden Triangle**
   * Disembark at the Civic Center / 13th & Broadway stop.
3. **Explore Art & History**
   * [History Colorado Center](https://www.historycolorado.org/history-colorado-center) (1200 Broadway) – Explore Colorado exhibits and interactive galleries.
   * [Denver Art Museum](https://www.denverartmuseum.org) (100 W 14th Ave Pkwy) – Stroll through the Hamilton Building and Martin Building galleries. Both venues remain open until 10:00 PM.

---

### Phase 3: Golden Triangle Return to Littleton
1. **Walk to Broadway & W 13th Ave** (1 block from Denver Art Museum / History Colorado).
2. **Board RTD Route 0 Bus (Southbound)**
   * Ride south along Broadway directly to **I-25 & Broadway Station** (~12-minute bus ride; buses run every 15–20 minutes).
3. **Transfer to RTD C Line Rail (Southbound)**
   * Board the southbound C Line train to **Littleton / Downtown Station**.

---

*Note: Final event shuttles depart museums at 10:00 PM. Have your RTD MyRide app or fare ready for RTD light rail and bus legs.*
